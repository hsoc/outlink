Outlink is an open-source Bluetooth mesh messenger for Android and iOS.

Chat publicly with nearby Bitchat devices or exchange encrypted private messages.
For internet conversations, join public location channels using an area code
(geohash). Optional foreground location access can select an approximate area;
manual area entry works without location permission.

No accounts, phone numbers, wallets, or registered usernames. Public mesh and
location-channel messages are visible to others. Location channels use internet
relays and are not encrypted private chats. Chat history expires after six hours. Drafts are saved locally until sent or cleared.
Android can keep Nearby connectivity active in the background when enabled in Settings. Remote push is unavailable.
