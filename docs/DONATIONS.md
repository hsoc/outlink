# Supporting Outlink

Outlink is free and open source. Donations are voluntary and do not affect app access or features.

- **Lightning Address:** `hsoc@coinos.io`
- **[Open the Coinos donation page](https://coinos.io/hsoc)**

Use a compatible Lightning wallet to enter the address or follow the Coinos link, choose an amount, and confirm the payment. The Lightning Address is reusable; do not publish a one-time BOLT11 invoice as a standing donation link.

In the mobile app, open **Settings → Support Outlink → Donate with Lightning**. This opens Coinos externally. You can also copy the address into your wallet. The app does not initiate, handle, custody, track, or verify a payment, and no donation is needed to use it. Payments require internet connectivity and a compatible wallet/payment service. Coinos and your wallet may process transaction information under their own policies.

Please verify the address shown in your wallet before approving a transfer; Lightning payments generally cannot be reversed.
