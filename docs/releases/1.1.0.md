# Outlink 1.1.0 Beta 1

App version **1.1.0**, Android version code **15**, iOS build number **15**.

## Changes

- Transfer prepared Cashu tokens through encrypted Nearby private conversations.
  Review the stated amount, mint and recipient before sending; retry interrupted
  transfers to the same recipient and copy received tokens to an external wallet.
- Keep Cashu tokens in encrypted records separate from chat history, with local
  protection against assigning overlapping proofs to another recipient.
- Move Location-channel activity, public relay visibility and identity-rotation
  explanations into an **Info** panel beside **Location channels**.

## Downloads and updating

- **Outlink-android.apk** is the signed Android installer.
- **Outlink-android.aab** is for Google Play Console, not direct installation.
- **SHA256SUMS.txt** lists checksums for both files.

Application ID: `io.github.hsoc.outlink`. Install over the previous signed beta to
retain local data. Debug-signed installations may require uninstalling first;
uninstalling removes local data, including any tokens without an external copy.
No iOS IPA is included in this release.

## Beta limitations

Cashu transfers require this feature on both Outlink phones. Funding and redemption
remain in an external Cashu wallet. **Received tokens are unverified until redeemed**;
offline transfers cannot prevent double spending. Outlink never contacts a mint or
reports payment settlement. Keep secure recovery copies until external redemption.
**Full erase, including the triple-tap shortcut, destroys stored tokens.**

Physical-device Bluetooth interoperability, signed-update installation and real-fund
Cashu redemption have not been tested. Delivery/background connectivity are not
guaranteed. Location channels are public to relay operators and listeners.
The dependency review retains 10 reported affected package entries (4 high,
6 moderate); no new reported entries came from the Cashu decoder dependencies.

See [Cashu instructions](https://github.com/hsoc/outlink/blob/v1.1.0-beta.1/docs/protocols/cashu-transfers.md),
[privacy](https://github.com/hsoc/outlink/blob/v1.1.0-beta.1/docs/PRIVACY.md), and
[dependency review](https://github.com/hsoc/outlink/blob/v1.1.0-beta.1/docs/security/dependency-review.md).
